Start in four steps

You can start with the demo, play a karaoke track you already have, or create an instrumental from a song.

1. Choose a track

Add an audio file from your device, or create an instrumental on the Karaoke Creator page.

2. Press Play

The Player shows the selected song. Drag the blue progress handle to jump to any point. Use Restart to play the current track from the beginning; Stop pauses it and returns to 0:00.

3. Tune it

Adjust key, tempo, backing volume, effects, or the sound balance while you listen.

4. Record if you like

Allow microphone access, sing with the backing, then listen to the take before downloading it.

Player: adjust while you sing

The Player and Creator share one player. When you choose a track, its name appears in the “Adjusting” label, so you can see which song the controls affect. You can download an adjusted karaoke track even if you do not record your voice.

How to use the Player: 1) Add or select a karaoke track in Your library. 2) Check the song title in Now playing and Adjusting. 3) Press Play and use the seek bar to move through the song. 4) Adjust key or pitch first, then tempo and sound effects while listening. 5) Tap Reset adjustments any time to return to the starting sound.

Choose a key or pitch

Use the key selectors to set the song’s current key and the key you want. The player shifts the track by the difference. If the original key is unknown, use the pitch slider or quick buttons by ear. Positive semitones raise the notes; negative semitones lower them. One semitone is one neighboring note.

Tempo and pitch

Tempo makes the song faster or slower. This player changes speed together with pitch, like speeding up a recording. Use key or pitch adjustment to move the song to a comfortable singing range. Make small changes and listen again.

Balance the instruments

Backing volume sets how loud the music is. Bass changes low sounds like kick and bass guitar; treble changes bright sounds like cymbals. Start with small moves to keep the backing clear.

Add space and stereo

Reverb makes the sound feel like a room or hall. Echo adds a short repeat. Pan moves the backing between left and right headphones; Center keeps it even in both ears.

Listen, then reset

Adjust while the song is playing so you can hear each change. If the sound gets confusing, tap Reset adjustments and begin again.

Repeat a tricky section

Play the song, tap Set A where practice should start, then Set B where it should end. The section repeats. Tap Clear to stop the loop.

Tip: Use headphones while adjusting and recording. This helps prevent the backing from leaking into the microphone.

Karaoke Creator: make an instrumental

Creator uses an audio separation model to reduce the lead vocal and make an instrumental version. The processing happens in your browser on your device.

1Choose Karaoke Creator

Select one song file. The chosen file name and ready status appear before you begin.

2Check the key estimate

The app may suggest an approximate Western major or minor key. Treat it as a hint and use your ears. It does not detect Indian ragas.

3Create karaoke

On first use, the browser downloads the free separation model (about 30 MB). Keep the page open and the device awake while it works.

4Return to the Player

The new instrumental is added to your library and selected for playback. Adjust it, sing along, or download the adjusted karaoke WAV.

For best results, create karaoke on a computer or tablet. Vocal removal can take several minutes and use a lot of memory. A phone may slow down or stop while processing. The Player, sound adjustments, and recording are designed to work on phones.

Separation is not perfect: a little vocal may remain, or some instrument sounds may be reduced. Results depend on the original recording.

Download karaoke without recording

In the Player, select a karaoke instrumental, adjust the settings, then tap Download adjusted karaoke (.wav) to save the adjusted track for your public performance. Recording your own voice is optional. The downloaded file contains the adjusted backing track, without a microphone recording.

If your song still has vocals: first use Karaoke Creator to make an instrumental. Then select that karaoke track in the Player, adjust the settings, and download the adjusted WAV for your public performance.

Record, listen, and keep your take

RECORD
VOICE + BACKING

Tap the large Record button and allow microphone access if asked. A large three-second countdown gives you time to get ready; the backing starts as recording begins.

  1. Sing while the selected backing plays.
  2. Tap Stop when you are finished.
  3. Use the recording preview to listen back.
  4. If you like it, download the take. Your take also appears under My recordings on this device.

The recording combines your microphone and the adjusted backing. Headphones usually give the clearest result. Recording requires a secure site (HTTPS) and microphone permission.

Display lyrics while you sing

Open Lyrics in the Player. You can paste lyrics or choose a text file.

Plain text (.txt)

Lyrics appear on screen for you to read. Plain text does not follow the song automatically, so scroll or follow along yourself.

Timed lyrics (.lrc)

An .lrc file includes time stamps. The matching line highlights as playback reaches it. Lyrics are not automatically transcribed from the song.

Your files, downloads, and privacy

Saved in this browser

Imported tracks, created instrumentals, recordings, and sound settings are saved in the browser on this device. They do not automatically sync to another phone or computer.

Downloaded files

Download buttons save an audio file to your device. Depending on your browser, it may ask where to save or use the default Downloads folder. Downloads are separate from the app’s local library.

Clear device library

Use Clear device library to remove tracks, instrumentals, recordings, and settings stored by the app on this device. It does not delete files already downloaded to your device.

Private processing

The app does not upload your song to its own server. An internet connection is needed for the first model download and for loading the karaoke engine.

Add the studio to your phone

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open the website in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If offered, turn on Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open the website in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (⋮).
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen, then follow the prompt.

If a previous version is installed, remove it and add it again after the website is updated so the new app name and icon appear.

Quick troubleshooting

Creator is slow or stops

Use a computer or tablet, close other apps and tabs, try a shorter audio file, keep the screen awake, and do not leave the page while processing.

No sound or wrong song?

Check the title in the Player and the “Adjusting” label. Choose the correct track in your library, turn up backing volume, and press Play.

Recording did not start

Allow microphone access in browser settings. Use the site over HTTPS, connect headphones, and check that another app is not using the microphone.

Where is my download?

Look in the browser’s Downloads list or your device’s Files/Downloads folder. A saved app library and a downloaded file are separate copies.

Key or raga seems wrong

The key estimate is approximate and only reports a Western major/minor key. It is not a raga detector. Dense mixes and songs that change key can confuse it.
